No heat or a leaking boiler: first steps

A boiler that stopped on a cold night is an emergency boiler repair in Bridgewater, MA, and our line is open 24/7. Check the thermostat, the breaker and the boiler's switch first. Look at the pressure gauge. Most home boilers run around 12 to 15 psi when cold, and a reading near zero means water has been lost.

If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 before you call us. Never relight a boiler that smells of gas. If you see water leaking from the boiler or the relief valve, turn the unit off and close the supply valve. Ask us about timing.

Gas boilers have an ignition and flame safety system. If the boiler locks out, there is a reason, and the reset is not a repair.

Gas safety first

  • ✓

    If you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 from outside.

  • ✓

    Do not use switches or phones inside, and do not relight the pilot.

  • ✓

    A carbon monoxide alarm that sounds means everyone leaves and calls 911.

  • ✓

    Soot, a yellow flame or a smell of exhaust means shut the boiler off and call us.

Gas fitting and oil burner work are licensed trades in Massachusetts. We tell you if your system needs a technician with a different license, and we do not make promises about gas leak response times.

Hydronic heating in older New England houses

Hot water boilers feed radiators or baseboards through a loop of pipe. A system that has run for decades often has scale, air pockets and tired circulators. Bleeding radiators, flushing the system and replacing a circulator are common repairs.

A boiler that is old, leaks from the heat exchanger or needs repeated repairs may be better replaced. We tell you the honest condition and explain what a replacement involves before you decide.

Boiler and hot water tied together

Some boilers also heat the household hot water through an indirect tank. If you have no heat and no hot water, one failure may explain both. If hot water fails and the heat works, see water heater repair.

A leaking boiler can also run up the water bill and stain a floor. If the source is unclear, leak detection finds it.
