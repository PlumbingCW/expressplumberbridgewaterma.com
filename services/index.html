Plumbing services in Bridgewater, MA split into two kinds of calls. Emergency calls are a burst pipe, a sewer backup, a failed water heater or a pump that stopped during a storm. Planned calls are installs, inspections and upgrades. Our line is open 24/7 for the first kind, and we book the second by appointment.

The services are listed below in the order people call about them. If you are not sure which one fits, start with the symptom. A slow drain points to drain cleaning, a meter that keeps turning points to leak detection and no hot water points to water heater repair.

Which page fits your problem

Water everywhere right now is a burst pipe or a water line problem, and the first step is the main valve. Sewage coming up through a floor drain is a sewer line problem, and a camera tells us whether the pipe is broken or just blocked.

A wet basement may be a failed pump or groundwater, which is why sump pump service and basement waterproofing sit side by side. A house with no heat in winter needs boiler repair.

Planned work includes water heater installation, tankless units, repiping and plumbing installation for remodels. Businesses can start with commercial plumbing.
