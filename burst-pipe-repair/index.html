When a burst pipe is an emergency

Water spraying from a split pipe is an emergency burst pipe repair in Bridgewater, MA, and our line is open 24/7. Close the main water valve first, usually where the line enters the basement near the meter. Then open the lowest faucet to drain the lines, and turn off power to any wet area at the breaker.

Move belongings out of the way, take photos of the damage for your insurer and call us. We ask about timing when you call, and we ask where the pipe is, what it is made of and how fast the water is flowing.

The first ten minutes

  1. Close the main water valve. If you cannot find it or it will not turn, call the town Water Department for the curb stop and call us.

  2. Open the lowest faucet and flush an upper toilet to drain the lines.

  3. Turn off the water heater's power or gas so it does not run dry.

  4. Switch off breakers to wet rooms. Do not stand in water near outlets.

  5. Photograph the damage, then start removing water with towels and buckets.

A shutoff that will not close is a sign the valve needs replacing. See faucet and valve repair for shutoff work.

Frozen pipes in a Massachusetts winter

Pipes freeze when they sit in unheated spaces such as exterior walls, crawlspaces, garages and basements near windows. Ice expands and splits the pipe, often at a joint, but the leak does not show until the ice melts and water flows. That is why a pipe can look fine in the morning and flood by noon.

If a pipe is frozen but not split, keep the faucet open, warm the area with a hair dryer or space heater and never use an open flame. If you suspect a split, close the main before you thaw. We repair the split and find out why it froze so it does not happen again.

Other causes of cracked and broken pipe

  • ✓

    Corrosion and pinholes in old copper or galvanized steel.

  • ✓

    Water hammer or high pressure stressing a joint.

  • ✓

    A failed soldered joint or a broken fitting.

  • ✓

    A pipe nicked by a nail or damaged during a remodel.

  • ✓

    Movement in the ground or house shifting a line.

Several pinholes in a copper system suggest the pipe is wearing out. In that case repiping can be better than patching each leak.

How we repair a burst pipe

We cut out the damaged section and replace it with new pipe of the same type or one that works with the old material, using fittings made for the job. Copper is soldered or joined with press fittings, and PEX uses crimp or expansion fittings. A repair on a drain pipe uses couplings sized to the pipe.

After the repair, we pressure test the line and open the water slowly to check for leaks. If the pipe is in a wall or ceiling, we make the smallest opening that works and tell you what finishing is left for you or a contractor. For a hidden leak, leak detection finds the spot first.

Insurance and what to document

Homeowners policies commonly cover sudden and accidental water damage, such as a pipe that burst, and commonly exclude damage from slow leaks or wear. Policies differ, so read yours and call your agent early. Take dated photos, keep the damaged pipe section if you can and keep receipts for emergency work and drying.

Ask your agent about water extraction and drying, since mold can start within a few days if materials stay wet. We tell you what we found and what we repaired in plain words so you can describe it to the insurer.
