Sewer backup and slow drains: what to do first

A drain that is fully blocked is an emergency drain cleaning job in Bridgewater, MA, and we answer the line 24/7. While you wait, stop running water in the house and do not flush. Water you add has to go somewhere, and if the blockage is in the main line it will rise in the lowest fixture, usually a basement floor drain or a first-floor tub.

Do not pour a chemical drain cleaner into a drain that is standing full. It sits in the pipe, it can burn skin when the plumber works, and it rarely clears a physical blockage. Tell us if you already used one so we can wear the right gear.

Ask about timing when you call, including after-hours visits. We ask how many fixtures are affected, because one slow sink and three backed-up fixtures point to different parts of the system.

One fixture or the whole house

The first question we ask is simple. Is it one drain or several? A single slow bathroom sink is usually hair and soap in the trap or the arm behind it. If the toilet, the tub and the laundry standpipe all gurgle together, the blockage is further down in the branch or the main line.

That split decides the tool and the price. A branch line clog is cleared with a cable through the cleanout or the fixture. A main line blockage may need a longer cable, a camera to see what is there, or hydro jetting when grease or roots keep coming back.

Kitchen sink clogs

  • ✓

    Grease cools and coats the pipe wall, then catches rice, coffee grounds and pasta.

  • ✓

    A garbage disposal pushes food into the same line, so a jam at the disposal can hide a clog past it.

  • ✓

    A dishwasher that backs up into the sink is a sign the blockage is past the shared drain.

  • ✓

    A slow sink that gets worse each week is partly blocked, not just slow.

If the disposal hums but will not turn, that is a separate repair covered under garbage disposal repair. Hot water and dish soap will not clear a grease plug that has hardened further down the line.

Snaking, cabling and what the cable reaches

Drain snaking uses a motor-driven cable with a cutting head. It breaks up a clog or hooks it so it can be pulled back. A hand auger works for a toilet or a short tub drain, and a drum machine handles lines running 50 feet or more.

Cabling cannot clean the full wall of a pipe. It opens a path. If the line clogs again within weeks, or the cable stops at the same spot each time, we recommend a camera look before another cleaning. That turns a repeat visit into a repair decision, and our sewer camera inspection page explains the process.

Sewer, septic and storm drains in Bridgewater

Roots, grease and wipes cause most blockages everywhere. What changes from house to house is the pipe and where it goes. Older cast iron and clay lines scale up and let roots in at the joints more readily than modern PVC. Bridgewater's building department asks for a paid water and sewer receipt on certain permits and, where none exists, an approved septic plan. That means some properties in town are on septic. A backup on one of those can start at the tank instead of the street main.

That is worth knowing before you call. We ask whether the house is on town sewer or septic, because clearing a house drain is a different job from a tank or leach field problem. If water is collecting at a yard catch basin or driveway drain, that is a drainage job, not an indoor clog, and clearing leaves and silt from the grate often fixes it.

Code and sources for this work

Drain and vent sizing in Massachusetts is set by 248 CMR 10.00, the Uniform State Plumbing Code, which is the standard we work to. Chemical drain openers are caustic and can harm pipes and skin, which is why we clear blockages mechanically.
