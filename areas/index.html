Plumbing Service Areas near Bridgewater, MA

We serve five nearby towns around Bridgewater. Each has its own water system, its own permit office and its own kinds of calls.

Call (508) 389-4045

Bridgewater uses ZIP 02324 and ZIP 02325. Our emergency line reaches the towns that border Bridgewater and a few close neighbors. Every town below has a page that explains its water system, its building department and the plumbing calls we see there.

Distances here are estimates and traffic changes them, so we never promise an arrival time. We do dispatch around the clock, and we plan the route when you give us the street and the nearest cross street.

Towns we serve around Bridgewater

West Bridgewater

ZIP 02379. Northwest of Bridgewater along Routes 24 and 106, with a published manganese notice and its own plumbing and gas division.

East Bridgewater

ZIP 02333. Northeast along Routes 18 and 106, with softened town water and a river-and-road layout.

Raynham

ZIP 02767. West of Bridgewater across the line in Bristol County, with separate water districts and sump pump calls.

Middleborough

ZIPs 02346 and 02344. South of Bridgewater, a very large town with mixed water and septic and boiler calls.

Halifax

ZIP 02338. East of Bridgewater around the Monponsett Ponds, with wet ground and service line work.

Other places we take calls from

We also take calls from Easton, north of Raynham, from Lakeville and Carver near Middleborough and from Plympton next to Halifax. These towns do not have their own page, and we tell you how we will route the visit when you call.

Inside Bridgewater itself, we have pages for the town center at Central Square, the Town River village of Stanley, the older village of Scotland and the homes near Lake Nippenicket.

Brockton is a larger city north of West Bridgewater and does not have its own page. If you are there, call with the address and we will tell you plainly whether we can reach you.
