Emergency Water Line Repair in Bridgewater, MA

The line from the street to your house is buried and out of sight. When it leaks or fails, you see it in the meter, the bill or a wet yard.

A failed water line is an emergency

No water, a soaked yard or a meter spinning with every tap closed can mean an emergency water line repair in Bridgewater, MA, and we answer 24/7. Close the main valve inside the house. If water still shows at the meter, the leak is between the meter and the valve, and the town may need to shut the curb stop.

Call us and say what you see. We ask where the line enters the house, how old the house is, what the pipe appears to be made of and whether the water pressure dropped before the leak. Ask us about timing.

Signs of a buried line leak

  • ✓

  • ✓

  • ✓

  • ✓

  • ✓

    The sound of running water when everything is off.

These are also leak detection signs, and leak detection pinpoints the spot before any digging starts.

Where the line runs and who owns what

A service line runs from the water main under the street to the meter or the main valve in the house. The utility owns the main and usually the connection. The homeowner is commonly responsible for the line from a certain point, such as the curb stop or the meter, to the house, but the exact split varies.

Call the Bridgewater Water Department to ask where your responsibility begins. The town's water page also lists a Lead Service Line Replacement Program with a grant, so ask whether your line is part of it before you replace it. We help you sort out what is yours and what is not.

A line that has been repaired several times usually needs replacement. If pipes inside the house are also failing, repiping may pair with a new service.

Low water pressure and the meter

Low pressure can come from the service line, a partly closed valve, a failing pressure reducing valve, scale in older steel pipes or a municipal issue. We test the pressure at an outside hose bib and at the main to find where it drops.

If the pressure is low at the meter too, ask the Water Department whether work or a restriction is affecting your street. The town has posted outdoor water use limits during a declared supply emergency, and its dashboard says water is blended through the distribution system, so check town notices before you assume the problem is on your side.

Code and sources for this work

Service lines are regulated by the local water department and by the state drinking water rules, 310 CMR 22.00. The EPA Lead and Copper Rule is the federal standard that now drives lead service line inventories and replacement.
