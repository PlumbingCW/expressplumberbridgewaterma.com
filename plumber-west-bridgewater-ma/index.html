Dark water and stained fixtures start the calls

Emergency plumber work in West Bridgewater, MA often begins with water that does not look right. The town's own water department posted a manganese notice in 2023 that explained how manganese, a mineral found in rock, soil and groundwater, can stain fixtures and turn water dark or cloudy when it is stirred up in the pipes.

That notice reported one test at a pumping station at 0.31 milligrams per liter in April 2023, just over the 0.3 guideline the state uses for infants, and it said a new treatment plant was being studied. Results change, so ask the department for current figures. What matters at your house is the plumbing it touches, because staining and sediment end up in tanks, aerators and toilet fill valves.

What manganese and iron do inside a house

  • ✓

    Black or brown staining in sinks, tubs and toilet bowls that scrubbing only partly removes.

  • ✓

    Sediment that collects in a tank water heater and shortens its life.

  • ✓

    Faucet aerators and showerheads that clog and cut the flow at one tap.

  • ✓

    Washing machine inlet screens that fill with grit.

A water heater that rumbles and runs out of hot water early is often carrying that sediment, and our water heater repair visits begin with a flush and a look at the anode rod.

How permits and licences work here

West Bridgewater's Building Department runs a Plumbing and Gas Division with a plumbing and gas inspector and an assistant inspector, and its page links to an online permit portal and to the state's license lookup. The town lists 248 CMR 10, the Massachusetts Uniform Plumbing Code, among the codes it enforces.

That means a water heater swap, a sewer repair or a new bathroom at a West Bridgewater address involves the town's inspector. A licensed plumber pulls the permit. Ask to see it and check the licence on the state's eLicensing search before you sign.
