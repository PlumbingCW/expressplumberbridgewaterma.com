East Bridgewater starts with its water system

A water heater, a tankless unit or a softener sees whatever the town's water carries, so we start there. The state's source water assessment for the East Bridgewater Water Department, dated December 2003, described five gravel-packed groundwater wells. It said the water is treated with lime slurry to soften it and with a product added to address manganese.

That report is more than twenty years old, and treatment can change, so ask the department for its current annual water quality report. What stays useful is the idea. Water that is softened and treated for manganese behaves differently in a tank than untreated water, and a plumber who knows the system can read the symptoms.

Rivers, roads and the way we reach you

If you have a burst pipe or active leak in East Bridgewater

Emergency dispatch for East Bridgewater is available around the clock. Do these four things while a crew heads your way.

Find the main

Turn the main water valve fully clockwise. Test a tap to confirm the flow has stopped.

Protect the electrics

Do not stand in water near outlets. Flip the breaker for any wet room if you can reach it dry.

Stop the heater

Turn the water heater to off or pilot so it does not heat an empty tank.

Photograph first

Take dated photos of the damage before cleanup, then call us with the address.
