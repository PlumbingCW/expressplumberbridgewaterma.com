What to do in a plumbing emergency before we arrive

  1. Close the main water valve, usually where the line enters the basement near the meter, and turn it fully clockwise.

  2. Switch off the breaker for any room where water is near outlets, and stay out of standing water that could reach them.

  3. Turn the water heater off at the gas control or the breaker so it does not run dry.

  4. Stop flushing and running drains if sewage is backing up, and keep people and pets out of the wet area.

  5. Take dated photos of the damage for your insurer, then call us with the address and what you see.

If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 from outside before you call a plumber.
