Jammed or leaking disposal: what to do first

A disposal that is leaking under the sink or backing up into the basin is an emergency garbage disposal repair in Bridgewater, MA, and our line is open 24/7. Turn off the switch and unplug the unit or switch off its breaker before you touch it. Never put a hand into a disposal.

If it hums but will not spin, the blades are jammed. Many units have a hex socket on the bottom for an Allen key, and a reset button next to it. If water is leaking, empty the cabinet and place a bucket under the unit. Ask us about timing.

Disposal symptoms and likely causes

SymptomLikely causeTypical fix
Hums, no spinJammed flywheelFree the jam with the hex key and reset
Nothing happensTripped reset or dead motorReset, check power, replace if the motor is gone
Leaks at the topFailed sink flange sealReseat the flange and replace the seal
Leaks at the side or bottomCracked housing or gasketReplace the unit
Slow drain through the unitClogged drain lineClear the line, then the unit

A leak from the body is usually the end of the unit. Leaks at the top or the pipe connections are often repairable.

When the clog is past the disposal

A disposal feeds into the same drain as the kitchen sink and often the dishwasher. If water fills both basins, the blockage is in the drain line, not the unit. Grease and food solids coat the pipe and trap more food.

We clear the line with a cable and check the disposal after. Our drain cleaning page covers kitchen sink clogs in detail. A dishwasher that backs up into the sink is a clue that the blockage is past the shared drain.

What not to put down a disposal

  • ✓

    Grease, oil and fat, which cool and coat the line.

  • ✓

    Coffee grounds, eggshells and starchy foods in large amounts.

  • ✓

    Fibrous foods such as celery, corn husks and artichokes.

  • ✓

    Bones, fruit pits and hard items that jam the blades.

  • ✓

    Anything that is not food, including utensils and bottle caps.

Run cold water before, during and after use. If the house is on septic, ask a septic pro about disposal use because it adds solids to the tank.

Dishwasher lines and the air gap

A dishwasher drains into the disposal or the sink drain. If the unit is not clear, the dishwasher can back up into the sink or leak at the connection. We reattach the dishwasher line and check the air gap or high loop that prevents backflow.

A new disposal installation includes the mounting, the electrical connection, the dishwasher knockout and the drain connection. If the sink is the problem, our faucet repair page covers the fixture side.
