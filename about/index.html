Why one number for every kind of call

Plumbing problems rarely arrive at a convenient hour. A pipe splits on a Sunday night, a water heater fails on the coldest morning of January and a sewer backs up before a holiday dinner. A single line that is answered at any hour means you never have to guess which office to try.

When you call, we ask for the address, what you can see and whether the main is shut. That is enough to give you the first steps and to plan the visit. If the problem is not urgent, we book the earliest suitable time and tell you plainly what to expect.

The jobs we handle

Our work falls into a few families. Drain and sewer jobs include drain cleaning, hydro jetting, camera inspections and sewer line repair. Water heater jobs run from a quick repair to a full replacement or a tankless install.

Leak and pipe jobs include leak detection, burst pipe repair, water line repair and repiping. We also handle sump pumps, boilers, fixtures and commercial buildings.

How we think about local information

Bridgewater has its own water supply, its own permit process and its own seasonal problems. The town draws its water from wells, the Building Department issues plumbing permits and winter freezes test every exposed pipe. We build our pages around those facts and cite town and state sources where we use them.

Where we could not find a primary source, we left the number out. A page that says nothing is better than a page that guesses, and you deserve to know which is which.

How we handle prices and surprises

A price is only useful if it holds. We give you the number before work begins, and we explain what is included. If we open a wall and find something that changes the job, we stop, show you and tell you the new price before going on.

That habit costs us the occasional quick sale, and it saves everyone from a bill that arrives with no explanation. It is also why we ask so many questions on the phone, since a good description makes the first price closer to the final one.

Licences and permits

Massachusetts licenses master and journeyman plumbers, and permits for plumbing work are issued by the local inspector to licensed plumbers. Minor repairs are exempt in many cases, but larger jobs such as a new water heater or a sewer repair are not. You can check any plumber's licence with the state Board before you hire them.
