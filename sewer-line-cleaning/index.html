Main line blockage: stop the water first

When the toilet, tub and floor drain all back up together, the main sewer line is blocked, and that is an emergency sewer line cleaning job in Bridgewater, MA. Our line is open 24/7. Stop running water, do not flush and keep out of any flooded basement area. The lowest drain in the house usually overflows first.

Call us and tell us which fixtures are affected. We ask if the house is on town sewer or septic, where the cleanout is and when the line last cleared. We ask about timing when you call and plan the cable length to the line.

Signs it is the main line, not a branch

  • ✓

    Several fixtures back up at the same time.

  • ✓

    Flushing a toilet makes water rise in a tub or shower.

  • ✓

    A basement floor drain gurgles or bubbles when upstairs water runs.

  • ✓

    The cleanout cap in the yard or basement shows standing water.

One slow sink is a branch problem and is covered under drain cleaning. A main line problem needs longer cable and sometimes a camera.

How we clear the main

We open the main cleanout and run a drum cable with a cutting head sized for the pipe. The head breaks through grease, paper, wipes or soft roots and clears the line as it is pulled back. For a line that is partly open and keeps clogging, we flush it with water to carry debris out.

When a cable cannot clear the line, or we suspect damage, we run a camera. If the pipe is cracked or collapsed, cleaning will not hold, and we say so rather than clear it and leave. See sewer line repair for those cases.

Wipes, grease and roots

Most main line blockages come from a few causes. Wipes labeled flushable do not break up like paper and collect on any rough spot. Grease coats the pipe wall and traps solids. Roots work through joints and spread into a mass.

The fix depends on the cause, so the cleaning visit includes a look at what comes back on the cable. If it is roots, you can expect a repeat unless the pipe is repaired. If it is grease, hydro jetting cleans the wall. If it is wipes, the habit is the fix.

Septic homes and town sewer homes

A house on septic has a different path. The house line runs to the tank and the tank to a leach field. A blocked house line is cleared the same way. If the tank is full or the leach field is saturated, that is a pumping or field problem and cleaning the line will not fix it.

If the house is on town sewer, the main also connects to the public line in the street. If water backs up and the cleaning does not help, the blockage may be in the town's main. Report it to the Department of Public Works. We tell you what we find so you can say it clearly.

Code and sources for this work

Cleaning does not change the pipe, so it follows the standard practice for the cable or jet equipment used. If a cleanout is missing, 248 CMR 10.00 governs how a new one is added.
