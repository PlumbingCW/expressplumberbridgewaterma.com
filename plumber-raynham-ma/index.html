Why the county line matters for a Raynham job

Raynham is in Bristol County, and Bridgewater is in Plymouth County. For you, that means a different town hall, a different inspector and a different set of water districts. We handle those differences. A plumber in Raynham must pull the permit from Raynham's own office, not Bridgewater's.

The town was incorporated on April 2, 1731 as a separate town from the eastern end of Taunton, and it keeps a separate identity from its neighbors. Routes 44, 104 and 138 cross it, Route 24 has an exit at Route 44 and Interstate 495 has an exit at Route 138. Route 24 and Interstate 495 meet right at the Bridgewater border, so our crews reach Raynham by highway.

Water districts, not one town system

Raynham has separate water districts. The state's assessment for the Raynham Center Water District lists eight wells, including Johnson Pond, the Nip wells and the Gushee Pond wells, with iron and manganese removal, chlorination and pH adjustment. A separate assessment exists for the North Raynham Water District.

Ask which district serves your street. Water treated for iron and manganese leaves less to settle in a heater, but any water that sits in a tank leaves some sediment. The protection area for the Nip and Gushee Pond wells extends into Bridgewater, so the same aquifer is under both towns.

Before the crew arrives in Raynham

Raynham dispatch is available around the clock. Use the minutes before we arrive on these steps.

Water in the basement

Stay out of water that could reach outlets, unplug what you can reach from dry ground and check whether the sump is running.

Leak upstairs

Close the main valve, open the lowest tap to drain the lines and switch off power to the wet room.

No water at all

Check whether neighbors are affected, since it may be a district issue, then call us with what you find.

Backed-up drains

Stop using water, keep drains closed and tell us which fixtures are affected.

Places around Raynham

Raynham Center is the town's main village, and North Raynham sits near the post office along Route 138. Raynham Park, once a greyhound track that held a record for the largest annual handle, closed live racing on January 1, 2010 and now hosts simulcast racing. A large building like that has its own plumbing demands, and our commercial plumbing page covers that scale of work.

We also take calls from nearby Easton. The Taunton River runs along the town, and low ground near it is where we see groundwater work.
