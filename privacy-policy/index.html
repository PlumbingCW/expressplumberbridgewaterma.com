Privacy Policy

How we handle information when you call or visit.

Call (508) 389-4045

Who we are

Bridgewater Express Plumbing operates this website and a plumbing phone line serving Bridgewater, MA. You can reach us at (508) 389-4045 or contact@expressplumberbridgewaterma.com.

Call recording notice

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you do not want a call recorded, tell us at the start of the call.

Information we collect

This site has no forms. We collect information you choose to give us when you call or email, such as your name, phone number, address and a description of the problem. Our website host and our analytics tools may collect basic technical data such as your browser type and pages visited.

How we use it

We use your information to answer your call, schedule and perform work, give you a price, follow up on a job and improve our service. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

We share what is needed with the plumbers who perform your service and with providers who help us run the phone line and the website. We may disclose information when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it or ask us to stop contacting you by calling or emailing us. We keep information only as long as we need it for the purposes above or as the law requires.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new version on this page with the date.

Our coverage area around Bridgewater, MA

Our line covers Bridgewater plus West Bridgewater, East Bridgewater, Raynham, Middleborough and Halifax.
