Active leak: what to do first

Water you cannot see is an emergency leak detection call in Bridgewater, MA when it is damaging a ceiling, wall or floor, and we answer the line 24/7. Shut off the main water valve to stop the flow. If the ceiling is bulging with water, poke a small hole to drain it into a bucket and keep clear of any light fixture or outlet below it.

Turn off power at the breaker to any room where water is dripping through a light or outlet. Then call us. Ask about timing when you call, including after-hours visits, and we ask where the stain is and when it started.

Meter still running, high water bill

  1. Close every faucet and shut off the ice maker, the irrigation system and the washer.

  2. Find your water meter and look at the leak indicator, a small dial or triangle that spins when water flows.

  3. If it spins, water is moving somewhere. Close the main valve and look again.

  4. If the meter stops with the main closed, the leak is inside the house. If it keeps moving, the leak is between the meter and the main valve.

  5. Call us with what you found so we can plan the search.

Many leaks are in the line between the street and the house. That is covered under water line repair.

How we find a leak without tearing it open

We start with the symptoms, a meter test and a pressure test on the lines. Then we narrow it with listening gear that picks up the sound of water escaping from pressurized pipe, moisture meters and a thermal camera that shows temperature changes behind finishes.

For drain leaks, a camera and a flood test can show where the water is escaping. Pinpointing the spot lets us open a small access hole instead of a large one. The repair then follows, with the right fix for pipe type and location.

If the cause is a failed fixture, our faucet repair and toilet repair pages cover that.

Slab, crawlspace and buried line leaks

Houses with a slab foundation, an addition or a slab garage can leak in lines that run under the concrete. Signs include a warm floor, the sound of running water when everything is off and a spike in the water bill. A leak under a slab is found with listening gear and tracer methods. Fixes include rerouting the line, a spot repair through the slab or repiping.

In crawlspaces and under-house areas, we look for damp insulation, dripping supply lines and wet soil. For buried lines in the yard, a wet patch, a soft spot or a lush strip of grass can mark the path. Irrigation lines, yard faucets and pool or spa fill lines are checked too.

Bridgewater's wet ground and your pipes

Bridgewater sits along the Taunton River, with Lake Nippenicket and part of the Hockomock Swamp Wildlife Management Area in the west. Low, wet ground raises the water table around those areas, and a damp basement does not always mean a pipe leak. We check the plumbing first, then tell you if the water is coming from outside.

If we find groundwater, not a leak, sump pump work or basement waterproofing may be the answer.
