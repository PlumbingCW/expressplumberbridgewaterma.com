A sump pump that stops during a storm leaves your basement with nowhere for water to go. We repair failed pumps and install new ones with battery backup.

Sump pump failure during a storm

A sump pump that has stopped while water rises is an emergency sump pump repair in Bridgewater, MA, and our line is open 24/7. Check the power first. Look at the plug, the GFCI outlet and the breaker. Lift the float to see if the pump starts. If it hums and does not pump, the impeller may be jammed.

Move belongings off the floor and unplug anything near water. Do not walk in standing water that could reach outlets. Call us and say how high the water is and whether the pit has a backup pump. Ask about timing when you call, including after-hours visits.

A pump that cycles constantly may be undersized, or the water table may be high. We look at both.

Why basements in this area see groundwater

Bridgewater lies along the Taunton River, with Lake Nippenicket and part of the Hockomock Swamp Wildlife Management Area in the western part of town. Low ground near water raises the water table, and after heavy rain or a long wet spell groundwater can push up into a basement.

That is why houses near those areas are the ones most likely to need a working pit, a good pump and a backup. A house that is dry for years can start taking water after a wet season. If it does, check the pump before you blame the pipes.

Battery backup and a second pump

  • ✓

  • ✓

    A second pump in the pit shares the load in a heavy storm.

The best setup depends on your pit and how often it runs. If the pump runs often or the basement is damp from walls or the floor, see basement waterproofing.

New pits, ejector pumps and discharge

A new sump installation starts with a pit below the slab, a pump with a float and a discharge line that carries water away from the house. The line needs a check valve so water does not drain back. Discharge location matters because water dumped near the foundation just returns.

An ejector pump is different. It lifts sewage from a basement bathroom to the main drain. If that pump fails, the toilet and sink may back up, and the repair is a different job. If the backup is from the sewer line, see sewer line cleaning.

Code and sources for this work

Sump discharge must be routed so it does not create a hazard, and 248 CMR 10.00 and local rules limit what may connect to a sanitary sewer. According to standard practice, a check valve belongs on every discharge line.
